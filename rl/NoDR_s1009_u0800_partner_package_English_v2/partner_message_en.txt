PPO updates. It includes the original TorchScript actor with frozen observation
normalization, a plain-PyTorch fallback, the exact73D input and16D action

velocity in the training coordinate frame. Its output must be converted to
absolute joint-position targets using the supplied decoder at a64ms policy period.
